{# Presses the media key Play/Pause: pauses or resumes music or video, in the browser, in Spotify and elsewhere.
With right-click › Set hotkey… the snippet gets a shortcut of its own, e.g. Ctrl+Alt+P, and then runs without the popup.
The settings list it under Hotkeys, the file name stays the same.
More special keys: volumemute, volumeup, nexttrack, prtsc, numplus on the numpad, key,a for a real key press.}
