{# Types a copied Excel row into a form: select cells side by side, Ctrl+C, click into the first field, pick the snippet.
Excel separates the cells with tabs. The script puts key('tab') in their place, which presses the Tab key and so jumps to the next field.
What comes from the clipboard never presses keys by itself. If it is empty, throw stops and a message says why.}
{{ if (!clipboard.trim()) throw new Error('Copy a row in Excel first'); clipboard.split(/\r?\n/)[0].split('\t').join(key('tab')) }}
