{# If Notepad is running, the snippet brings it to the front and opens a new tab. Otherwise it starts Notepad, where the tab is empty already.
if,process=notepad checks whether a Notepad window is open and then brings it to the front like window. If there is none, it goes on after else.
What follows /if runs in both cases. The Notepad of Windows 11 swallows characters when typing fast, so delay,30 types more slowly.}
{if,process=notepad}
{ctrl+n}
{else}
{start,notepad}
{/if}
{delay,30}Note of {{ date('dddd, MMMM d, yyyy') }}, {{ time() }}{enter}{enter}
