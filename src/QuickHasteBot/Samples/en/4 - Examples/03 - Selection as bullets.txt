{# Turns the selected lines into a list with bullets, empty lines are left out.
Handy for lists from Excel, emails or notes: select the lines, pick the snippet, done.
With nothing selected, a single bullet comes to write on.
For a numbered list, there is "04 - Selection as a numbered list".}
{{ selection.split(/\r?\n/).map(line => line.trim()).filter(line => line).map(line => '• ' + line).join('\n') || '• ' }}
